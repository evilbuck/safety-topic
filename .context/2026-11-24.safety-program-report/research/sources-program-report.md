## S1. Cal/OSHA 8 CCR §3203 — official DIR text
URL: https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/3203.html (fetched 2026-11-24; page history notes end at Register 2020, No. 10)

§3203(b): "Records of the steps taken to implement and maintain the Program shall include:

(2) Documentation of safety and health training required by subsection (a)(7) for each employee, including employee name or other identifier, training dates, type(s) of training, and training providers. This documentation shall be maintained for at least one (1) year.

EXCEPTION NO. 1: Employers with fewer than 10 employees can substantially comply with the documentation provision by maintaining a log of instructions provided to the employee with respect to the hazards unique to the employees' job assignment when first hired or assigned new duties.

EXCEPTION NO. 2: Training records of employees who have worked for less than one (1) year for the employer need not be retained beyond the term of employment if they are provided to the employee upon termination of employment.

EXCEPTION NO. 3: [employers <20 employees, non-high-hazard + EMR ≤1.1, or <20 in low-hazard industries] written documentation of the Program may be limited to … C. Written documentation of training and instruction as required by subsection (a)(7)."

§3203(a)(8)(B)(1): employee access to the Program "in no event later than five (5) business days after the request for access is received"; (a)(8)(C): "The Program provided to the employee or designated representative need not include any of the records of the steps taken to implement and maintain the written Program."

§3203(c)(2) (committee route): "Prepares and makes available to the affected employees, written records of the safety and health issues discussed at the committee meetings and, maintained for review by the Division upon request."

## S2. Cal/OSHA Compliance Manual Chapter 13 — Enforcement of IIPP
URL: https://www.dir.ca.gov/dosh/policies-and-procedures/ch-13-enforcement-of-iipp.pdf (fetched 2026-11-24; redirect target of the older P&PC-45A URL)

Authority: "California Code of Regulations, Title 8, Sections 336 and 3203 • California Labor Code, Section 6319, 6401.7, and 6428.5"

Section II.A: "CSHOs must assess all eight IIPP elements during the course of the inspection through interviews as well as requesting relevant documents via the Cal/OSHA Form 1AY: Document Request."

Section II.B: "A request for documents must include records required to be maintained as part of the IIPP, including but not limited to, scheduled and periodic inspections, safety and health training records, disciplinary records, accident/incident investigation forms, written procedures, or safety committee meeting minutes."

Section II.7 sample effectiveness measure (Training): "Is there a sign-in sheet?"

Section II.9 (Recordkeeping), verbatim: "Records of safety and health training, including employee name or other identifier, training dates, the type(s) of training, training providers, and whether, for employers with ten or more employees, the records have been maintained for at least three years. EXCEPTIONS: … i. For employers with fewer than ten employees, a log of instructions can be maintained which provided instructions to the employee with respect to the hazards unique to the employee's job assignment when first hired or assigned new duties."

Section II.9 sample effectiveness measure: "Does employer have records of all scheduled and periodic inspections? Health and safety training records? Investigations conducted as a result of an incident or accident? Safety meetings?"

NOTE (discrepancy): the regulation text (S1) says one (1) year; this enforcement manual (current revision, served 2025/2026) evaluates "at least three years" for employers with ten or more employees. Both texts captured above; the manual's criterion is the Division's inspection posture, not an amendment to the CCR (the official DIR §3203 page still reads one year).

Section IV.B: industries with IIPP-specific standards — "§ 1509 – Construction … When an employer in the above industries has violated its specific IIPP standard, CSHOs must cite the particular 8 CCR section and reference the applicable subsection of § 3203."

Section V.A.5: "An employer subject to §1509 shall conduct 'toolbox' or 'tailgate' safety meetings, or the equivalent, with their crews at least every 10 working days to emphasize safety. CSHOs shall reference in the citation how the employer failed to comply with §1509(e)."

## S3. Cal/OSHA 8 CCR §1509 — Cornell LII text
URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/8-CCR-1509 (fetched 2026-11-24)

"(a) Every employer shall establish, implement and maintain an effective Injury and Illness Prevention Program in accordance with section 3203 of the General Industry Safety Orders.

(e) Supervisory employees shall conduct 'toolbox' or 'tailgate' safety meetings, or equivalent, with their crews at least every 10 working days to emphasize safety."

NOTE: §1509 contains no recordkeeping subsection of its own; the record duty arrives via (a) → §3203(b).

## S5. Washington WAC 296-155-110 — Cornell LII text
URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/washington/WAC-296-155-110 (fetched 2026-11-24)

"(5)(a) You must hold crew leader-crew safety meetings at the beginning of each job, and at least weekly thereafter.

(6)(d) You must document attendance. (6)(e) You must document subjects discussed.

(7) You must prepare minutes of each crew leader-crew meeting and you must maintain a copy at the location where the majority of the employees of each construction site report for work each day.

(8) You must retain minutes of crew leader-crew safety meetings by the employer for at least one year and you must make them available for review by personnel of the department, upon request."

Note in rule: "Subcontractors and their employees may, with the permission of the general contractor, elect to fulfill the requirements of subsection (5)(a) and (b) … by attending the prime contractors crew leader-crew safety meeting."

## S12. OpenReplay — How to Create a Downloadable File in the Browser (secondary, practitioner)
URL: https://blog.openreplay.com/create-downloadable-file-browser (fetched 2026-11-24)

Canonical pattern: "new Blob([data], { type }) → URL.createObjectURL() → an anchor with the download attribute → click() → URL.revokeObjectURL()."

data: limits: "Current data: URL size limits are 512 MB in Chromium and Firefox and 2048 MB in Safari/WebKit (MDN data: URL reference)" — plus Base64 +33% overhead; Blob recommended.

iOS Safari: "iOS Safari has historically had unreliable support for files generated and downloaded via the download attribute (WebKit bug 167341), so a generated file frequently opens in a new tab or renders inline instead of saving." Workarounds: keep the save synchronous with the user gesture (no await/setTimeout before click); give a "tap Share → Save to Files" hint.

Excel/CSV: "Excel opens a UTF-8 CSV file correctly when the file is saved with a BOM, so prepend one to the string" (`\uFEFF`; Microsoft Support link in article). RFC 4180: CRLF record delimiter; quote fields containing commas/quotes/newlines, double internal quotes. "using application/octet-stream to force downloads can cause issues in Safari."

Download attribute history: "supported in Chrome 14+ and Firefox 20+ (caniuse: download attribute)."
