## Cal/OSHA "Setting up a Tailgate/Toolbox Safety Meeting" (Jan 2020) (s25, PDF read)
- "They are required for the construction (T8CCR Section 1509) and tunneling (T8CCR Section 8406) industries."
- "all California employers must have an Injury and Illness Prevention Program that includes employee training in safe work practices (T8CCR Section 3203). Tailgate/Toolbox meetings can help reinforce that training."
- How often: "Section 1509 (construction) requires them at least every 10 working days, and Section 8406 (tunneling) at least weekly. However, depending on the work conditions and safety culture of the job site, they should be held more frequently – even daily if necessary."
- "Keep the meeting short – usually 10 to 15 minutes."
- "Document/record the meeting topic, date, attendees, and any actions taken."
- Mentions "Tailgate Safety Meeting Topics worksheet for selecting, tracking, and recording the tailgate meeting topics."

## 8 CCR §3203 (IIPP) (s20, LII read)
- (a)(3): communication system; "Substantial compliance… includes meetings, training programs, posting…"
- (a)(7): training triggers (new hire, new assignment, new hazard, etc.).
- (b)(2): "Documentation of safety and health training… for each employee, including employee name or other identifier, training dates, type(s) of training, and training providers. This documentation shall be maintained for at least one (1) year." Exceptions: <10 employees may keep a log of instructions; small/low-hazard employers may limit written docs.
- (c)(2): committee records "maintained for review by the Division upon request… at least one (1) year."

## PRISM "Best Practices for Tailgate and Toolbox Safety Meetings" (s12, PDF read)
- Recites §1509 (every 10 working days), §8406 (weekly; "Records of all meetings shall be kept, stating the meeting date, time, personnel present, subjects discussed, and corrective actions taken if any, and maintained for inspection by the Division"), §3203.
- "Weekly meetings should be very in-depth…"
- "Agencies should keep records on all safety meetings… a sign-in sheet stating the meeting date, time, employees present, subjects discussed, and the trainer's name. A separate sign-in sheet should be used for each time a safety meeting is conducted and for each topic that is discussed."
- "PRISM (through consultation with Cal/OSHA) recommends maintaining all documentation for at least 3 years or longer. This ensures that an agency has the required documentation to present to Cal/OSHA if a request for documentation is made."
- "PRISM also recommends that agencies… conduct a daily 5 to 10 minute safety briefing with employees at the start of each shift."

## Oregon OSHA guide 0989 "Safety committees and safety meetings" (s17, PDF read)
- "If you have construction workers: Meet at least monthly and meet before the start of each job that lasts more than one week. If your employees do mostly office work: Meet at least quarterly. All other employers: Meet at least monthly."
- "If your employees do construction, utility work, or manufacturing, you must keep minutes of all safety meetings for three years. All other employers: You do not have to keep minutes as long as all your employees attend…"
- Minutes fields: Date, Attendees' names, safety/health issues discussed incl. hazards; official minutes form has Attending/Absent lists.
- Committee: monthly/quarterly as above; 3-year records; minutes available to all employees.

## Streak research (s21, author-hosted PDF; s22)
- Mehr, Silverman, Sharif, Barasch, Milkman, OBHDP 187 (2025) 104391, open access CC BY.
- Abstract: "we test 'streak incentives,' or rewards that offer people increasing payouts for completing multiple consecutive work tasks. Across six pre-registered studies (total N = 4,504)… people actually complete more work when compensated with streak incentives than with larger, stable incentives… the requirement that they complete work tasks consecutively" drives goal commitment → persistence.
- Cites Silverman et al. 2023: "people are more likely to continue engaging in that behavior when they have an intact (vs. broken) streak of logging it."
- Streaks defined as "sequences of three or more consecutive tasks (Carlson & Shu, 2007)."
- ScienceDirect landing page returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare) — author PDF used instead.
