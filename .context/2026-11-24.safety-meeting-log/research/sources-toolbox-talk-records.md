# Sources: toolbox-talk documentation requirements (accessed 2026-11-24)

- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.21 — 29 CFR 1926.21(b)(2) hazard-instruction duty.
- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/1997-08-14 — "no standard that requires the employer to obtain the employee's signature… employer and trainers to sign a certification record which includes the identity of the person(s) trained."
- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/2000-04-10 — training standards expect employer/trainer-signed certification records; e-signatures acceptable.
- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/2010-04-28 — Training Standards Policy Statement: verify training happened and was understandable.
- https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/1375-agreement-20250910.pdf — partnership agreement: weekly toolbox talks, document number of employees trained (search-index excerpt; direct fetch 403).
- https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/3203.html — CA 8 CCR §3203(b)(2): documented training with employee identifier, training dates, types, providers; ≥1 year retention. Verified verbatim.
- https://www.saif.com/documents/SafetyandHealth/SafetyCommittee/S574_Safety_meeting_minutes.pdf — SAIF S574 safety meeting minutes (fields snippet-sourced; PDF fetch failed — partial verification).
