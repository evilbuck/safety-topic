# Notes: toolbox-talk documentation requirements

### OSHA federal position
- 29 CFR 1926.21(b)(2): employer must instruct employees in recognition/avoidance of unsafe conditions — no documentation format specified.
- OSHA interpretation letters (1997-08-14, 2000-04-10): no federal standard requires employee signatures; training standards expect the **employer/trainer to sign a certification record identifying who was trained**. Electronic signatures acceptable.
- OSHA Training Standards Policy Statement (2010-04-28): officers verify training happened and was understandable; look beyond basic paper documentation.
- OSHA construction partnership agreements require documented weekly toolbox talks ("document the number of employees trained").
- Confidence: high (osha.gov laws-regs pages and interpretation letters, several direct).

### State law and practical drivers
- California 8 CCR §3203(b)(2): IIPP training documentation **required** — employee name/identifier, training dates, type(s) of training, training providers; retain ≥1 year. Verified verbatim from dir.ca.gov.
- Insurers/GCs/auditors treat records as proof; retention recommendations run 1–3+ years (Workyard, SALUS).
- Confidence: high for CA §3203; medium for retention norms (secondary sources).

### Conventional sign-off sheet fields (template survey)
Convergence across SAIF S574, SALUS, Workyard, ConstructionBids, TailgateDocs:
- Header: date, topic title, presenter (+title), location/job site, company, duration (min)
- Attendance: printed name + signature per row; TailgateDocs attestation line "By signing I confirm I attended this safety talk and understood its content."
- Footer: notes/follow-up actions, presenter signature + date (the one signature OSHA actually expects)
- Typical sheet: one page, ~10–15 signature rows.
- Confidence: high (multiple independent templates); SAIF field list snippet-sourced only (PDF fetch failed).

### Toolbox-talk app landscape
- Safesite: conduct-meeting flow per project; completed meetings viewable/filterable by date/topic; PDF export.
- Safetymint: schedule/document/track; digital attendance per session.
- TailgateDocs: pure print generator — fill fields, print, hand around.
- Confidence: high (product pages read directly).

### Bearing on SafetyTopic
The app dispenses the brief but stops before the record. A printable sign-off sheet (brief + header + attendance + attestation + presenter sign-off) plus a dated local log of delivered talks turns "topic picker" into "run and document your safety talk" — the documentation gap every surveyed tool fills and CA §3203 explicitly requires.
